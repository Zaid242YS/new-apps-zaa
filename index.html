The Creed-to-Commerce™ Business Toolkit

1.

Business Funding Navigator and Manual

aims to help fund your business on a foundation you can stand behind with confidence. Here’s how this happens. Most owners don't fail for lack of a good business idea. They fail because they adopted the wrong financing at the wrong time. The Business Funding Navigator walks you through five stages (Creed based on Tawheed, Clarity based on sound Islamic values, a comparison based on solving problems for customers, a contract based on shared vision, and commerce based on all activities stemming from Creed (Tawheed) This Creed to Commerce Connection business link stands before you ask anyone for money to conduct business.

You enter your real numbers, and see Murabaha, Ijarah, Musharakah and Mudarabah compared side by side on cost and risk-sharing, and get a best-fit structure with the operating steps to follow. Then you can print a Funding Findings Report, an Operations Brief, and a one-page Client Snapshot to put in front of a partner, banker, or board to elicit all parties having clarity.

The companion manual shows you how to use every manual screen with confidence. This stops guessing and it starts funding with peaceful clarity.

2.

Footprint Ledger and Manual

is designed to build the daily discipline that makes the money work and it opens the door for revenue (Funding) and it compiles daily habits to keep the business alive. The Journal Footprint Ledger is a private, passcode-protected, 90-day entry ledger that turns scattered business days into a visible trail of decisions, actions, and results.

You record each day's steps, see your patterns, and you own the whole journey instead of wondering where the month went. When you want proof of progress, one tap on the menu produces a PDF report. Your entries stay on your device, so your business stays private.

The user guide shows you how to turn 90 days of footprints into steady momentum. Small steps, tracked daily, become a business footprint you can trust.

3.

Problem Solving Workbook App and Manual

is developed to fix the money leaks that become losses. Every business has leaks and cash that disappears, debt that tangles, decisions made under pressure. The Problem Solving Workbook is a 61-page interactive money-mindset and budgeting system built for small business owners.

Start with the quiz to get your day one score, a business profile, and recommended worksheets. Then use the live calculators for Cash Flow, Profit-First Split, Emergency Buffer, Debt Detangler, and Decision Matrix to see your finances emerge.

Retake the quiz on Day 30 and measure your progress. Back up your work, print it filled or blank, and build a PDF report whenever you need one. The manual walks you through it, page by page. Fix what's draining you and build a calmly profitable business.
